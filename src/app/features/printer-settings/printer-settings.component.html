How printing works

  • Veg KOT prints only veg items on your Veg KOT printers; Non-Veg KOT prints only non-veg items on Non-Veg KOT printers. Use KOT (All Items) for a printer that should receive the full order regardless of food type.
  • Customer Bill prints on all Bill printers — a full tax invoice with item breakdown and totals.
  • Both documents are sent automatically — no manual action needed.
  • Supported: 80 mm & 58 mm ESC/POS printers via Bluetooth (Xprinter XP-58/80, Epson TM-T20, TVS RP-3200), USB cable (OTG adapter on phones), or Network/WiFi (Epson TM-T82X, TM-T88, any printer with port 9100 on your LAN).
  • You can add the same printer to both pools to receive both KOT and Bill.
